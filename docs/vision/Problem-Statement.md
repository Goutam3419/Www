# Problem Statement

## Purpose

Articulate the problem AI CEO Studio exists to solve, distinct from the
vision statement, so scope decisions can be traced back to a concrete pain
point.

## Scope

The problem space for a non-technical or time-constrained founder trying to
turn an idea into working software. Does not cover enterprise engineering
teams or existing-codebase modernization — out of scope for v1.

## Problem

Turning a product idea into a working, deployed application still requires
assembling and coordinating a team: someone to plan, someone to design,
someone to write backend and frontend code, someone to review it, someone to
test it, and someone to deploy and operate it. Existing AI coding tools
accelerate one person in that chain — typically the coder — but leave the
founder to play every other role manually, including the coordination between
them.

AI CEO Studio's premise is that the coordination itself is the bottleneck, not
just the code-writing, and that a defined multi-agent workflow with explicit
human approval gates can remove that bottleneck without removing the founder's
control over the outcome.
